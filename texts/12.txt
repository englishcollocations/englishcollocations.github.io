Lesson 12 – Computers

I'm teaching my grandmother how to use a computer. First I showed her how to <strong>start up</strong> and <strong>shut down the computer</strong>. Then, I helped her <strong>get online</strong> or <strong>access the internet</strong> so that she can <strong>check her e-mail</strong>. She quickly learned how to <strong>write and send messages</strong>, <strong>reply to an e-mail</strong> and <strong>forward an e-mail</strong> (send a complete e-mail that you received to a new person), but it was a little hard for her to remember how to <strong>attach a file</strong> to an outgoing message.

We tried to <strong>log into</strong> her bank account online, but the bank's website was <strong>down</strong> (the website was not functional, or was not online). There was a notice on the <strong>home page</strong> (the main page of the website) that they were doing some <strong>scheduled maintenance</strong> on the system – so we'll try again tomorrow.

This afternoon, I'll teach her how to use a <strong>search engine</strong>. I think she'll really enjoy <strong>surfing</strong>/<strong>browsing the web</strong> (exploring the internet) and using <strong>social media</strong>. If she needs to do something more advanced, like <strong>burn a CD</strong> (put files from a computer onto a CD), then I can do that for her.

She's a little bit afraid of pressing the wrong key and accidentally <strong>deleting an important file</strong>, but I told her that we can always <strong>recover the file</strong> from the <strong>recycling bin</strong>. I also explained that if the computer <strong>freezes</strong> or <strong>locks up</strong> (stops moving/functioning), she can simply <strong>restart</strong>/<strong>reboot the computer</strong>.

It's also a good idea for her to <strong>back up her files</strong> (make an extra copy of the information) so that she doesn't <strong>lose her data</strong> if the computer <strong>crashes</strong> (the computer breaks) and can't be repaired. I installed a program that periodically <strong>runs scans</strong> for <strong>viruses</strong> and <strong>spyware</strong> to ensure that her computer hasn't been <strong>infected</strong>.

Lesson 3 – Appearance

My best friend Joanna is <strong>absolutely gorgeous</strong> (extremely beautiful). She has long, <strong>sleek</strong>, <strong>jet-black hair</strong> (sleek = smooth and straight, jet-black = perfectly black), <strong>pale blue eyes</strong> (pale = light color), and a <strong>radiant complexion</strong> (skin that appears healthy and full of energy).
People always compliment her on her <strong>hourglass figure</strong> (when a woman has large breasts and hips, but a small waist) and ask her what the secret is to maintaining such a <strong>slender waist</strong> (a thin waist). She has a <strong>round face</strong> with an <strong>upturned nose</strong>, and she actually <strong>bears a striking resemblance</strong> (looks extremely similar) to a famous singer.
Joanna could get any guy she wanted – so I was really surprised when she introduced me to her latest boyfriend, who I think is <strong>hideously ugly</strong>. He has curly, shoulder-length hair that looks rather <strong>unkempt</strong> (unkempt hair = messy hair, it appears that the person doesn’t take care of their hair).
He has a <strong>square face</strong> and a <strong>ruddy complexion</strong> (reddish skin). His <strong>deep-set eyes</strong> (eyes that are far back in the person’s face) are almost hidden under his <strong>bushy eyebrows</strong> (big eyebrows with lots of hair), and he sports a <strong>thick mustache</strong> and a huge, <strong>shaggy beard</strong> (a beard with lots of hair). I guess Joanna doesn’t mind his <strong>facial hair</strong> (facial hair = beard and/or mustache).
His body isn’t bad – he has an <strong>athletic build</strong> (body with a lot of muscles), with <strong>broad shoulders</strong> (wide shoulders) and <strong>muscular arms</strong>. If he would only <strong>trim his beard</strong> (cut his beard a little shorter) and comb his hair, I suppose he could be considered <strong>somewhat attractive</strong> (more or less beautiful/handsome).

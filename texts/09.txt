Lesson 9 – Music

My older brother Alex is very <strong>musically gifted</strong> (he has a natural talent for music). He can <strong>play any tune</strong> from memory, without looking at <strong>sheet music</strong> (musical notation written on paper). He spends all his free time <strong>strumming a guitar</strong> (playing a guitar), <strong>composing music</strong> (creating music), and writing <strong>song lyrics</strong> (the words to a song).

Alex and his friends <strong>formed a band</strong> that has attracted a <strong>sizable following</strong> (a lot of fans) in our city. They started out <strong>playing gigs</strong> (giving small, informal performances) in bars and clubs, and they used the money they earned to record their <strong>debut album</strong> (record their first CD).

A <strong>remixed track</strong> (a song that has been modified electronically) from that CD then became a <strong>massive hit</strong> (a very popular song) on the local radio station thanks to its <strong>catchy tune</strong> (memorable melody) and <strong>upbeat tempo</strong> (fast rhythm).

Their second album will be <strong>released</strong> (it will be made available to the public) at the end of the year; they'll <strong>record it live</strong> at their next show. Alex dreams of <strong>going on tour</strong> – he's just waiting for his <strong>big break</strong> (big opportunity to become famous) to <strong>get his foot in the door</strong> of the music industry.

As for me, I love <strong>singing along</strong> (singing together) to my brother's music, but a career in music isn't for me – I'm completely <strong>tone-deaf</strong> (unable to correctly identify different musical notes) and I always <strong>sing off-key</strong> (sing the incorrect melody)!

Additional collocations:
• <strong>contemporary music</strong> = modern music
• <strong>classical music</strong> = traditional orchestral music
• <strong>orchestral music</strong> / <strong>instrumental music</strong> = music with instruments only (no singing)
• <strong>rock music</strong> = popular music with strong rhythm
• <strong>solo album</strong> = CD featuring one singer alone, not in a group
• <strong>compilation album</strong> = a CD with a collection of songs from many different singers/bands
• <strong>cult following</strong> = a very dedicated/devoted group of fans

Lesson 2 – Relationships

When you move to a new city, it can be hard to <strong>make friends</strong>. You’ll probably have a number of <strong>casual acquaintances</strong> – people who you know on a basic level, but with whom you don’t have a significant relationship. Over time, some of these may become <strong>good friends</strong> or <strong>close friends</strong> – these expressions refer to friends with whom you have an especially strong relationship.
Do you believe in <strong>love at first sight</strong>? That’s when two people meet each other and immediately fall in love – perhaps because they have <strong>strong chemistry</strong> – this expression describes strong compatibility or connection between two possible romantic partners. Some people believe that their <strong>significant other</strong> (their boyfriend, girlfriend, husband, or wife) is their <strong>soul mate</strong> – this is a poetic expression that means that two people are destined to be together.
It’s possible for one person to <strong>have feelings for</strong> another person – this expression is an indirect way to describe romantic feelings – but if the other person doesn’t feel the same way, then the <strong>feeling is not mutual</strong>. The other person might say “he’s/she’s just <strong>not my type</strong>” – meaning that they are interested in people of a different type of personality/appearance. Ah, there’s nothing sadder than <strong>unrequited love</strong>! (Love that is not returned)
Some people marry their <strong>childhood sweetheart</strong> or <strong>high school sweetheart</strong>/<strong>college sweetheart</strong> – this expression refers to a boyfriend or girlfriend from your childhood, teenage, or college years. Other couples are introduced by a <strong>mutual friend</strong> – one friend that the two people have in common. When the couple is ready to <strong>make a commitment</strong>, the man <strong>proposes to</strong> the woman – he asks her to marry him. If the marriage is good, then we say the couple is <strong>happily married</strong>.
Unfortunately, not all love stories have a <strong>happy ending</strong>. Sometimes a husband or wife <strong>has an affair</strong> – they have sex with a different person. We can also say that they are <strong>cheating on</strong> their husband/wife. The expression “have an affair” is typically used only for married couples, and the expression “cheating on” can be used for married couples or for boyfriends/girlfriends.

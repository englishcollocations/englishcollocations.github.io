Lesson 20 – City

When my parents retired last year, they traded the <strong>towering skyscrapers</strong> (very tall buildings) and <strong>noise pollution</strong> (excessive noise) of Los Angeles for the peace and quiet of a small <strong>coastal city</strong> (a city near the ocean) in southern France. They said they were tired of living in a <strong>high-rise building</strong> right next to an <strong>industrial zone</strong>. Now they live in a cozy little house on the <strong>outskirts of the city</strong> (on the outer edge of the city, not in the center).

Life in their new town is quite different from the <strong>hectic pace</strong> (the fast and chaotic rhythm) of L.A., where the <strong>bustling streets</strong> (busy streets filled with people) are <strong>strewn with litter</strong> (filled with garbage) and you can count on spending hours in <strong>traffic jams</strong> (stopped traffic).

Instead, the narrow <strong>cobblestone streets</strong> of the <strong>city center</strong> are lined with eighteenth-century buildings that are now home to <strong>quaint shops</strong> (charming and old-fashioned shops) and gourmet restaurants specializing in the <strong>local cuisine</strong> (the typical food of the region).

The best day to visit is Saturday, when there's an <strong>open-air market</strong> (an outdoor market) in the main square. You can buy souvenirs from the <strong>street vendors</strong> (people selling things on the street) and have lunch at one of the <strong>trendy cafés</strong> (modern, stylish, and popular cafés) in the area. There's virtually no <strong>street crime</strong> here, in contrast to the <strong>inner-city areas</strong> (older, central, crowded part of a city, usually filled with poor people and possibly violence) of L.A.

Is there anything that my parents miss about Los Angeles? Well, they do miss the <strong>cultural diversity</strong>. But they're past the age where being in a place with a <strong>pulsating nightlife</strong> (having many exciting bars and clubs) is important to them.

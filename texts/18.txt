Lesson 18 – Travel

Last week I had the worst <strong>business trip</strong> I've ever taken. Normally a <strong>travel agency</strong> takes care of <strong>booking my flight</strong> (buying a plane ticket) and making my <strong>hotel reservation</strong>, but this time I decided <strong>make the travel arrangements</strong> myself. Big mistake!

First of all, the plane was <strong>overbooked</strong> (there were more passengers than seats available) so I was <strong>bumped to a later flight</strong>. When they gave me my new <strong>boarding pass</strong> (the ticket that allows you to enter the airplane), they didn't ask me if I'd prefer a <strong>window seat</strong> or an <strong>aisle seat</strong> (a seat next to the corridor) – and I was stuck in the middle. To top it off, it was a very <strong>bumpy flight</strong> (a flight with a lot of turbulence).

I then <strong>missed my connecting flight</strong> and had a ten-hour <strong>layover</strong> (I had to wait ten hours for my next flight) in Chicago. Unfortunately, my <strong>checked luggage</strong> (the bags in the airplane's cargo compartment) had already gone through to my final destination, so all I had was my <strong>carry-on bag</strong> (the bag you take with you on the plane). I <strong>checked into a hotel</strong> near the airport just so I could take a nap.

When I finally arrived in Los Angeles, it turns out that I had made a mistake with my <strong>hotel reservation</strong>, and instead of booking a room at a <strong>five-star hotel</strong>, I had booked three nights at a <strong>seedy hotel</strong> (a hotel that is not very safe or comfortable) in the <strong>red-light district</strong> (an area of the city where there are many sex shops, strip clubs, etc.)! So I was stuck sleeping on an uncomfortable mattress in a <strong>dingy room</strong> (a dirty and dark room) while my colleagues enjoyed the <strong>luxury hotel</strong>.

The trip home was uneventful, but when I arrived I discovered that the airline had <strong>lost my luggage</strong>. All in all it was a miserable experience.

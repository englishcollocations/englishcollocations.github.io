Lesson 21 – Countryside

If you're living in an urban area and looking for a little change of scenery, why not take a weekend trip to the surrounding countryside? You can walk through the <strong>wildlife preserve</strong> (special area where animals are protected), where a <strong>winding footpath</strong> (a road you can walk on, that has many turns and changes of direction) will lead you through varied landscapes including <strong>dense forests</strong>, <strong>vast meadows</strong>, and gentle, <strong>rolling hills</strong>.

More adventurous visitors will enjoy exploring the <strong>rocky landscape</strong> of Yosemite National Park with its <strong>towering cliffs</strong> (very tall cliffs) and <strong>magnificent waterfalls</strong>. You can take a <strong>hiking trail</strong> that goes up the <strong>steepest face</strong> of the tallest mountain in the park (the side of a mountain is called a "face" – and "steep" means it is very vertical). The trail starts at the <strong>foot of the mountain</strong> (the base of the mountain) and the ascent takes about four hours; you can stop to have lunch on the <strong>banks</strong> of a clear, <strong>bubbling mountain stream</strong>.

From the <strong>summit</strong>, there's a <strong>spectacular panoramic view</strong> (meaning you can see in all directions) of the area. To the east, you can see the jagged, <strong>snow-covered peaks</strong> of a distant <strong>mountain range</strong> (a group or chain of mountains); you can gaze at the <strong>lush green countryside</strong> (an area with lots of healthy vegetation) to the south, and when looking west, you can see the slow-moving, <strong>shallow river</strong> (a river that is not very deep or fast) that <strong>flows lazily</strong> towards the ocean.

The coastline is just a three-hour drive from the mountains, with miles of <strong>sun-drenched</strong>, <strong>sandy beaches</strong>. You can stay overnight at a <strong>family-run hotel</strong> in the <strong>picturesque fishing village</strong> (beautiful, good for taking pictures), where every room has an <strong>unobstructed view</strong> (there are no obstacles blocking the view) of the sea. The beaches tend to get crowded in the summer, so one option is to take a boat trip across the bay and explore the <strong>secluded beaches</strong> (beaches that are hidden) on the island – it's <strong>well worth the trip</strong> (meaning it's a good use of your time to go there).

  <li><strong>renewable resources</strong> – natural materials that can be replenished naturally.<br><em>Example:</em> Water, wind, and sunlight are renewable resources for energy production.</li>

  <li><strong>developing countries</strong> – nations with lower levels of economic development.<br><em>Example:</em> Many developing countries struggle with inadequate healthcare systems.</li>
  <li><strong>food shortages</strong> – insufficient supply of food for a population's needs.<br><em>Example:</em> Drought and conflict have caused severe food shortages in the region.</li>
  <li><strong>lack of access to clean water</strong> – inability to obtain safe drinking water.<br><em>Example:</em> Lack of access to clean water affects billions of people globally.</li>

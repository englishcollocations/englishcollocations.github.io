  <li><strong>soul mate</strong> – a person who is perfectly suited to another in temperament and destiny.<br><em>Example:</em> They believe they are soul mates.</li>
  <li><strong>have feelings for</strong> – to have romantic emotions toward someone.<br><em>Example:</em> He has feelings for his coworker.</li>
  <li><strong>the feeling is not mutual</strong> – the other person does not feel the same way.<br><em>Example:</em> I liked her, but unfortunately the feeling was not mutual.</li>

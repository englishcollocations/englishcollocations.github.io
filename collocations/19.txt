  <li><strong>heavy rain</strong> – intense, abundant rainfall.<br><em>Example:</em> The heavy rain caused flooding in several neighborhoods.</li>
  <li><strong>torrential rain</strong> – extremely heavy, violent rainfall.<br><em>Example:</em> The torrential rain made driving dangerous and visibility poor.</li>

  <li><strong>constant rain</strong> – uninterrupted rainfall over time.<br><em>Example:</em> The constant rain ruined our camping trip plans.</li>

  <li><strong>The wind is dying down</strong> – wind speed is decreasing.<br><em>Example:</em> The wind is dying down, so we can finally light the campfire.</li>

<ol>
  <li><strong>lifelong fan</strong> – someone who has liked something for their whole life.<br><em>Example:</em> I’m a lifelong fan of U2.</li>
  <li><strong>all-time favorite</strong> – favorite of all time.<br><em>Example:</em> They’re my all-time favorite band.</li>
  <li><strong>sheer enjoyment</strong> – pure pleasure.<br><em>Example:</em> In addition to the sheer enjoyment of listening to their music.</li>
  <li><strong>great admirer</strong> – someone who has great respect or admiration for someone.<br><em>Example:</em> I’m a great admirer of Bono.</li>
  <li><strong>fleeting pleasure</strong> – momentary enjoyment.<br><em>Example:</em> Not only concerned with the fleeting pleasure of fame.</li>
  <li><strong>hold (someone) in the highest regard</strong> – have great respect for someone.<br><em>Example:</em> I hold him in the highest regard.</li>
  <li><strong>particular liking</strong> – a special fondness for something.<br><em>Example:</em> She developed a particular liking for classical music.</li>
  <li><strong>real pleasure</strong> – genuine happiness.<br><em>Example:</em> It was a real pleasure to see her progress.</li>
  <li><strong>obvious enjoyment</strong> – enjoyment that is clear to see.<br><em>Example:</em> Her obvious enjoyment of the lessons.</li>
  <li><strong>grow quite attached</strong> – to become fond of someone or something over time.<br><em>Example:</em> She grew quite attached to her teacher.</li>
  <li><strong>great love</strong> – strong affection or passion for something.<br><em>Example:</em> Her great love of music.</li>
  <li><strong>clear preference</strong> – a definite choice or liking for something.<br><em>Example:</em> She’s expressed a clear preference for difficult pieces.</li>
  <li><strong>instant dislike</strong> – dislike felt immediately.<br><em>Example:</em> I took an instant dislike to Sam.</li>
  <li><strong>deep dislike</strong> – very strong dislike.<br><em>Example:</em> His deep dislike of foreigners.</li>
  <li><strong>nothing but contempt</strong> – complete hatred.<br><em>Example:</em> He had nothing but contempt for them.</li>
  <li><strong>blind hatred</strong> / <strong>irrational hatred</strong> – hatred without logical reason.<br><em>Example:</em> Sam’s blind hatred of foreigners.</li>
  <li><strong>strong aversion</strong> – strong dislike.<br><em>Example:</em> He developed a strong aversion to interacting with others.</li>
  <li><strong>hate (someone) with a passion</strong> – hate someone a lot.<br><em>Example:</em> I hate him with a passion.</li>

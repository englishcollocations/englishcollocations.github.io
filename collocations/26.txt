  <li><strong>time-consuming tasks</strong> – activities that require a lot of time to complete.<br><em>Example:</em> Data entry and research are often time-consuming tasks.</li>
  <li><strong>pressing commitments</strong> – urgent obligations or appointments that cannot be delayed.<br><em>Example:</em> She couldn't attend the meeting due to pressing commitments at home.</li>

  <li><strong>get ahead</strong> – complete work earlier than planned or required.<br><em>Example:</em> Try to get ahead on your assignments during spring break.</li>
  <li><strong>falling behind</strong> – becoming late or delayed compared to the planned schedule.<br><em>Example:</em> The team is falling behind on their quarterly sales targets.</li>
